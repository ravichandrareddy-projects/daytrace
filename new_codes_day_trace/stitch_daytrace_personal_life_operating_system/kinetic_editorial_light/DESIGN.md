## Brand & Style

This design system translates the deliberate pacing, rigorous structure, and focused intensity of high-end editorial tools into a high-visibility, daylight-first interface. Designed specifically to pair seamlessly with its dark-mode sibling, it shifts from nocturnal introspection to crisp daytime velocity. 

The aesthetic is Modern Editorial: disciplined typographic hierarchies, generous white and porcelain breathing room, structural slate divider lines, and purposeful, high-chroma semantic accents. It rejects visual clutter, excessive skeuomorphism, and heavy drop shadows in favor of balanced surface layering, razor-sharp edge contrast, and micro-interactions calibrated for speed and focus.

Targeting self-directed knowledge workers, writers, and quantitative planners, the UI evokes clarity, composure, and forward kinetic momentum. High outdoor contrast and uncompromising legibility are prioritized across all daylight conditions.

## Colors

The color palette is built around pristine porcelain foundations and disciplined slate tonalities, punctured by deliberate semantic accents:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50 / Soft Porcelain) creates a soft, paper-like background that mitigates screen glare.
  - Surface elevated / Container: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) provides crisp separation for cards, modals, and focal zones.
  - Recessed / Inset surfaces: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) provides structure for table headers, segment controls, and input backings.
- **Borders & Rules:**
  - Structural divider: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) delivers crisp, low-fatigue structural boundaries.
  - Interactive border: `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) denotes unselected actionable bounds.
- **Typography & Text:**
  - Primary text: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) guarantees maximum contrast against white and porcelain planes.
  - Secondary / Supporting: `#334155` (Slate 700) balances reading comfort with high legibility.
  - Muted / Tertiary: `#64748B` (Slate 500) designates metadata, timestamps, and placeholder copy.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - **Deep Indigo (`#4F46E5` / Hover `#6366F1`):** Serves as the primary driver for key actions, link treatments, and system focus.
  - **Calm Emerald (`#059669`):** Designates completed milestones, positive status indicators, and verified states.
  - **Active Amber (`#D97706`):** Highlights active sessions, ongoing tasks, and editorial revisions requiring immediate attention.

## Typography

Typographic treatments rely solely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, tall x-height, and open counters to maintain precision and supreme outdoor daylight visibility. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked with heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to evoke an editorial publication masthead. They demand focus without feeling heavy-handed.
- **Body:** Standard letter-spacing with open, calculated line-heights ensures sustained reading comfort during continuous writing and task parsing.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-contrast, semi-bold to bold weights with gentle tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) ensure interface metadata, status pills, and keyboard accelerators are immediately decipherable at quick glances.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an adaptable 12-column layout on desktop, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs a centered max-width workspace (max 1440px) with `2rem` side margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Wide gutters ensure complex views (such as multi-pane editorial logs and analytical sidebars) maintain clear spatial boundaries.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Collapses secondary inspector panels into side-drawers, preserving a comfortable reading column width with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Drops to `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Elements reflow to a pure single-column hierarchy; action toolbars anchor to safe bottom zones for thumb reach.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** All interior spacing follows an 8pt architectural rhythm, using `space-xs` (4px) for micro-alignments, `space-sm` (8px) for icon-label gaps, `space-md` (16px) for card body padding, and `space-xl` (40px) to separate primary document sections.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid murky, heavy shadows in bright environments, depth is created primarily through **tonal surface separation** backed by razor-thin slate outlines and warm, low-opacity ambient shadows:

1. **Flat / Base Level:** `#F8FAFC` base plane with no shadow. Structural dividers rely on `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
2. **Elevated Cards & Panels (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces perched over `#F8FAFC`. Structured with a fine perimeter border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow tinted slightly toward cool slate: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Popovers (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF` dropdowns and context menus receive a sharper outline (`1px solid #CBD5E1`) and a floating ambient shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
4. **Modals & Dialogs (Level 3):** Centered `#FFFFFF` containers backed by a warm light scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.35)` with an optional `backdrop-blur: 4px`), framed with a crisp border and deep elevation: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced `ROUND_EIGHT` standard (`0.5rem` / 8px). This geometry matches the structured precision of editorial publication layout while introducing subtle softness for tactile touchpoints.

- **Standard Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to buttons, text inputs, action badges, and nested content blocks.
- **Containers & Large Panels (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px):** Used on overarching section cards, modals, and canvas panels to create a self-contained feel.
- **Pills / Fully Rounded (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for semantic tags, quick-filter chips, and compact numeric counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#4F46E5`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, radius `8px`. On hover: `#6366F1` with an ambient glow (`0 4px 12px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.25)`). On active: `#4338CA`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `8px`. On hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#334155`. On hover: background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Tag:** Height `24px` to `28px`, radius `9999px`, padding `0 10px`, font `label-md`.
- **Positive / Done:** Background `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`, text `#059669`, border `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`.
- **Active / In Focus:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`, text `#D97706`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.
- **Neutral:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Form Controls
- **Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `8px`, vertical padding `10px`, horizontal padding `14px`. Placeholder text `#94A3B8`. On focus: border `#4F46E5`, outline `2px solid rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size `18px`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio), border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state: background `#4F46E5`, border `#4F46E5`, checkmark `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Grouping
- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border-radius `12px` to `16px`, padding `space-md` to `space-lg`. Hoverable timeline entries transition border to `#CBD5E1` with elevation Level 1.
- **Metric / Summary Card:** Recessed variant with background `#F1F5F9` and border none to present secondary summary statistics without visual competition.

### Lists & Timeline Entries
- **List Rows:** Divided by clean `1px solid #F1F5F9` rules. Hover triggers a subtle `#F8FAFC` row illumination.
- **Kinetic Timeline Node:** Left-hand vertical track in `#E2E8F0` with `8px` circular progress nodes. Active milestones utilize `#D97706` with an amber pulse; completed segments transition to `#059669`.
